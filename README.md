What ICT means to me.
To me, Information and Communication Technology isn’t just textbook definition, but it is the base of how we build, solve, and connect with others online.

Whether it's writing code to bring a circuit to life or using tools like GitHub and Gmail to collaborate, technology is a practical toolkit. It’s taking raw logic and turning it into real-world solutions. At the end of the day, ICT gives us the power to not just use digital systems, but to create and shape them ourselves.
